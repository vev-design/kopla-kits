# Design trend notes

Short-lived notes on visual/product directions worth watching when
authoring or refreshing a kit. These are prompts for kit authors, not a
spec — nothing here changes the contract in `../CONTRACT.md`.

## Week of 2026-09-01 — dark glass, kinetic type, grain

- **Dark glassmorphism** — near-black/deep-purple grounds with neon-blue
  and hot-pink accents, behind frosted, translucent panels (blurred glass
  over ambient gradients rather than flat dark UI). No kit currently
  targets this look; `deck` is dark but flat/high-contrast, not glass.
- **Kinetic, variable-weight typography** — headlines whose weight and
  scale shift with scroll, type carrying pages that would otherwise lean
  on imagery. Already the premise of the `kinetic-type` kit — treat this
  as a continuing direction, not a gap to fill.
- **Grainy, "imperfect" textures** — deliberate noise/grain over otherwise
  clean digital surfaces, as a counterpoint to flat vector polish.
- **Audio as a design deliverable** — sound treated alongside image and
  motion in prototypes/deliverables; more relevant to demo tooling than
  to static kit output.
