# AI Product

A conversion-first design system for AI and developer products, in the classic
dark platform look — the Vercel / Linear / model-lab aesthetic. The canvas is a
neutral near-black, the ink near-white, and the primary action is a confident
white button; a single electric-iris accent is held in reserve for glows,
gradient edges, and the highlighted plan. What makes the system read as
engineered rather than decorated is its terminal voice: Geist carries headings
and body while Geist Mono carries the hero's prop-driven terminal window,
uppercase eyebrows and cell micro-labels, the hairline "used by" table, and the
code-comment testimonial attribution. Layouts are deliberately asymmetric —
a left-aligned split hero, a bento capability grid, hairline-ruled strips —
and faint masked grids sit behind the hero and closing CTA to suggest a
product built on infrastructure. This kit is dark by design — there is no
light counterpart.

This is a marketing page system, not a presentation deck. Every section is a
fluid, full-width band that a downstream caller fills through props: a sticky
navbar with in-page anchor links, a split hero pairing benefit copy with a
terminal window (or screenshot), a mono wordmark table, a bento capability
grid, a code-comment customer voice, usage-based pricing, a closing call to
action, and a structured footer. Motion is deliberately restrained — tasteful
scroll-in reveals and gentle staggers — because the goal is to move a
developer toward signing up, not to dazzle them.

## Composition

The chain mirrors how a high-converting developer-platform page builds trust
and pushes toward signup. **Navbar** anchors the page and keeps the primary CTA
in reach at all times; its links jump to the section ids below. **Hero** states
the core promise above the fold with left-aligned copy beside a live-feeling
terminal window — commands, output, and a blinking cursor, all prop-driven —
over a faint grid and an iris glow, so the value proposition and the "this is
a real tool" signal land together. **LogoCloud** immediately follows with
low-commitment social proof rendered as a tight hairline-ruled table of mono
wordmarks — a status readout, not a marketing band — reassuring the skeptic
before they read on.

With trust seeded, **FeatureGrid** does the explaining as a bento: the lead
capability anchors a large 2×2 cell (with room for a visual), four singles
fill in beside it, and wide half-row cells extend below, each cell tagged with
a mono micro-label so the grid scans like a spec sheet. **Testimonial** then
converts explanation into belief with a single engineer's voice — a
left-border-accented quote attributed in monospace like a code comment.
**Pricing** arrives once the visitor is convinced, presenting usage-based
tiers with one plan lifted by the iris ring to guide the choice. **CTA** is
the closing push: a gradient-edged card with its own grid and glow that
restates the offer with nothing to distract from the button. **Footer** closes
the page with navigation, legal, and the wordmark — the calm landing after the
pitch. The rhythm alternates dense and sparse — split hero, thin wordmark
table, dense bento, intimate quote, structured pricing, single-message CTA —
so the eye keeps moving toward the conversion.
