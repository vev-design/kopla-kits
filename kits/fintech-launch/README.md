# Fintech Launch

Fintech Launch is a polished business-finance presentation system built for products that make ambitious companies feel calm, capable, and ready to act. It uses deep black stages, fog-grey operational surfaces, quiet 18px corners, spacious Plus Jakarta Sans display typography, and deliberately simple acquisition actions. It is suited to business accounts, embedded finance, accounting tools, and company-formation services where clarity needs to feel premium rather than ornamental.

The foundation is intentionally single-scheme. Black is the dramatic stage for big promises and social proof; #F5F5F5 is the working surface for product explanations; #E8E8E8 creates the softer tile layer between them. Every reusable action and surface is token-led, while media arrives through editable content slots rather than being baked into the layout.

## Composition

The sequence begins with a tall, full-bleed Hero that establishes confidence before asking for action. It passes directly into ProductMosaic, where a dark, asymmetrical bento grid makes the account offer feel immediately operational: one large overview tile anchors the story while smaller tiles resolve specific team and automation benefits. ProofRow deliberately removes visual density for a moment of credibility before InvoiceGrid moves the story into a lighter, calmer product workflow.

IntegrationSpotlight follows as a visual bridge from individual workflows to the wider tool ecosystem. CompanyCreation shifts the product’s scale toward a founder’s concrete next milestone, and CustomerStory returns to a black, immersive frame so an actual customer voice can land with weight. EarlyAccessForm provides a focused native email-capture moment for launches and waitlists, with its confirmation designed into the same stage. ClosingCta resolves the narrative in one uncompromising action; Footer then leaves practical links and brand context without competing with the conversion moment.

## Catalog

The component catalog contains the kit’s own Button, Badge, Card, and Nav primitives, all with explicit variants and static showcase states. Sections compose these rather than recreating their UI treatments inline. Motion remains CSS-first: the navigation carries its soft color transition, the mosaic’s accent card uses a restrained rotating gradient, and proof points reveal on scroll through the provided static motion wrapper. Display and wordmark tracking remain open enough to keep large lettering crisp at every size.
