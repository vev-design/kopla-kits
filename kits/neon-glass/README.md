# Neon Glass

A dark, ambient design system built around one idea: the interface itself is
a lit surface. A near-black canvas holds slow, oversized blooms of deep
violet and electric blue; frosted glass panels — heavy backdrop blur, a
hairline white border — sit on top of the glow so it reads through the
material instead of just behind it. Hot pink is the one loud accent, held in
reserve for the primary action and the recommended plan so it still lands as
an accent rather than noise. A fine fractal-noise grain sits over the whole
page, cutting the shine of pure digital gradients so the glass feels tactile
and slightly imperfect rather than sterile. Headlines are set in Bricolage
Grotesque, a genuinely variable display face, and sweep font weight from
hairline to bold as they cross the viewport — a pure-CSS scroll effect that
turns typography into the hero image, no photography required. This is a
dark-by-design system; there is no light counterpart.

This is a marketing/announcement page system for brands that want spectacle:
AI and creative-tool launches, music and event pages, app or drop reveals,
premium tech products. Every section is a fluid, full-width band filled
through props — a frosted sticky navbar, a full-bleed ambient hero with the
kinetic headline, a bento grid of glass feature cards, a single glass-framed
media showcase, a row of glass stat tiles that count up on scroll, a glass
testimonial, glass pricing tiers with one ring-lit highlight, a closing
gradient CTA, and a glass footer.

## Composition

The chain builds the way a lit room reveals itself: you see the glow before
you see the furniture. **Navbar** opens translucent and sticky, so the
gradient underneath is visible through it from the first scroll. **Hero**
is the signature beat — three ambient color blooms behind a single frosted
panel, with the headline's variable weight sweeping from thin to bold as it
enters view, carrying the drama a hero image would in a flatter system.

With the tone set, **FeatureGrid** breaks the pitch into a bento of glass
cards — one large anchor cell backed by five supporting ones — each pairing
a glowing icon chip with a short claim, so the system explains itself in
scannable pieces rather than paragraphs. **Showcase** then does the one
thing a bento grid can't: holds a single large screenshot or clip inside a
gradient-edged glass frame, the "look at the thing" moment the chain has
been building toward. **StatRow** follows with a quick, glowing proof of
momentum — three tiles whose figures count up as they enter — before
**Testimonial** turns that momentum into belief with one voice in a glass
panel. **Pricing** arrives once the visitor is convinced, its recommended
tier lifted by the same hot-pink ring used everywhere else emphasis is
needed, so the ring itself becomes a learned signal by the time it reaches
the plan that matters. **CTA** is the brightest moment in the system — every
color bloom used elsewhere overlaps once, behind one message and one button
— and **Footer** lets the glow settle into a calm, structured close. The
rhythm alternates dense and sparse throughout — bento grid, single
showcase, quick stats, intimate quote, structured pricing — so the eye
never sits still for long, on brand for a system that's meant to feel alive
rather than static.
