# Proposal

Proposal is an editorial system for a document addressed to ONE named audience and argued top to bottom. It sets deep forest ink, a paper-like off-white canvas, acid-lime signals, and warm peach accents. DM Sans carries the story with bold, comfortably tracked display moments that stay legible at presentation scale, while the imagery stays human: teams, daylight, and living landscapes rather than product screenshots.

Its voice is practical and collective. The demo content frames a workplace climate programme as a benefit people can join rather than a compliance initiative, which is the shape the system is tuned for: a proposal that argues for participation rather than announcing a decision.

## Composition

The story begins in a dark, image-led hero, making the customer and the programme’s emotional proposition immediately legible. A fixed thumbnail rail sits outside the narrative flow, making each later beat immediately reachable without competing with the hero. The three-pillar strategy section then turns that feeling into a credible employee-benefit argument, with structured space for the buyer to understand why participation will work.

An editorial image collage opens the page back up and lets the team-centred principles circulate as social proof. The dark rollout sequence slows the pace into three practical programme beats, using a sticky image to give implementation a human scale. A high-contrast closing invitation turns the narrative into a working-session ask, while the compact footer provides a clean handoff.

## Motion notes

Reveal and staged entrance motion use the kit’s CSS-only wrappers. The principle rail adds a slow 45-second linear drift; other interactive surfaces keep restrained transform, colour, and border transitions.
