# Finance

A data-forward design system for a trustworthy money product — an investing
app, digital bank, or wealth platform. The look is light, crisp, and precise:
deep ink type on a flat white ground with a faint cool-green cast, a single
confident emerald-teal accent (`#0E8C6A`) used sparingly, and a small-to-medium
radius that reads as composed rather than playful. There are no gradient
washes — sections sit on plain surfaces separated by hairline borders, so the
page feels like a fintech product, not a template. Inter carries the interface
while JetBrains Mono with tabular numerals is the system's data voice: the
hero's headline figure, the ticker strip, metric rows, prices, and every
uppercase eyebrow render in mono, so numbers line up and read unmistakably as
engineered data. The result feels like a regulated institution that happens to
be beautifully designed — calm, credible, and built to earn confidence.

Numbers and charts are first-class elements of this system, not an
afterthought. The hero is a split composition: left-aligned copy beside a
framed data panel — a mono label, a tabular headline figure with an emerald
delta chip, and a portfolio-growth chart — closed at the bottom edge by a
thin ticker strip of label + value stat pairs separated by hairlines. A
dedicated performance band stacks growth and returns charts beside headline
figures, and the chart blocks inherit the emerald palette so data
visualizations re-skin with the brand. Every editable value flows through
props, each section is a fluid full-width band, and motion stays restrained —
tasteful scroll-in reveals and gentle staggers — because the goal is to move a
visitor toward opening an account, not to dazzle them.

## Composition

The chain is a confidence-building narrative tuned for a financial product,
where trust must be earned before the ask. **Navbar** anchors the page and
keeps "Open account" in reach at all times. **Hero** states the core promise
beside a framed data panel — headline figure, delta chip, portfolio-growth
chart — so the value proposition and the evidence for it land together above
the fold, and its ticker strip hands off a first row of hard stats at the
section's bottom edge. **Metrics** immediately deepens that with full-size
figures — assets, accounts, uptime, fees — rendered in mono so they read as
fact, not marketing.

With scale established, **Features** does the explaining: an icon-led grid that
breaks the platform into scannable capabilities. **Performance** is the
evidence section and the system's signature beat — charts of growth and annual
returns beside headline figures and an honest disclaimer, the proof a money
brand lives or dies by. **Security** follows in a deliberately sober register,
reassuring the skeptic on encryption, regulation, and protection before any
commitment is asked. Only then does **Pricing** present transparent, all-in
tiers with one recommended plan to steer the choice. **CTA** is the closing
push — a single emerald band that restates the offer with a reassurance note
and nothing to distract from the button. **Footer** lands the page with
navigation, social, copyright, and the regulatory disclaimer a financial
product is expected to carry. The rhythm alternates dense and sparse — hero,
metric row, feature grid, chart-led proof, trust grid, structured pricing,
single-message CTA — so the eye keeps moving toward the conversion.
